José Alek Nava Sánchez (“Adlek”, “we”, “us”), located at Hidalgo #25-B, Xaloztoc, Tlaxcala, Mexico, is responsible for the personal data you provide to us. This policy is issued under Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP) and covers our advertising management and marketing services, including our software application that connects to Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google and customer relationship management (CRM) platforms (the “Services”). We use data only to serve you and to plan, run, measure and report on campaigns for the businesses that hire us. We do not sell personal information.

1.Information we collect

This policy applies to:

  • People who contact us through WhatsApp, social media, forms on our website or other channels, and visitors to our website.
  • Clients: businesses that hire Adlek and connect their ad accounts, Pages, pixels or CRM to our Services.
  • Leads and website visitors of our clients: people who see our clients’ ads, visit their landing pages, fill out a form or chat with them.

When we process data about a client’s leads and visitors, we act on behalf of, and under the instructions of, that client, as a service provider (data processor). The client’s own privacy policy also applies.

When you contact us

  • Identification and contact data: name, phone number (including your WhatsApp number), email address and profile name.
  • Content of your communications: messages, voice notes, images and documents you send us.
  • Service-related data: the service you are interested in, appointment dates, the channel you came from and your interaction history.
  • Technical data: message date, time and delivery status, obtained through messaging platforms.

From platforms our clients connect

  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram): ad account, campaign, ad set, ad and creative settings; performance metrics (impressions, clicks, spend, conversions); Page and Business Manager identifiers; pixel and Conversions API event data.
  • CRM platforms (for example GoHighLevel / LeadConnector): lead and contact records, such as name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, form answers, pipeline stage and the ad or campaign that generated the lead.
  • Google (Analytics / Ads): aggregated traffic and conversion data.

From landing pages and websites we manage

  • Information a person submits in a form or chat, for example name, phone, email, address or ZIP code, and project details.
  • Technical and event data collected by the Meta Pixel, Google tags and similar technologies (see section 8).

From our clients directly

  • Contact and billing details, business information, creative assets and the instructions they give us.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (for example health, ethnic origin, beliefs or financial data) through these channels. Please do not send it to us. If any of our services requires sensitive data, we will ask for your express written consent before processing it.

2.How we use information

Primary purposes, necessary for the service:

  1. To answer and follow up on your messages, requests and questions.
  2. To schedule, confirm, remind you of and follow up on appointments or meetings.
  3. To provide the services you hire and give you support.
  4. To run automated assistants (chatbots) that answer your messages and, when needed, hand you over to a person.
  5. To create, manage, optimize and report on advertising campaigns for our clients.
  6. To send conversion events (such as a submitted form) to advertising platforms, including through the Meta Conversions API, so they can measure and optimize ads.
  7. To deliver leads to our clients’ CRM and help them follow up with the people who contacted them.
  8. To build dashboards, reports and insights for our clients.
  9. To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve our Services, and to comply with legal obligations and platform policies.

Secondary purposes, not necessary for the service:

  1. To send you information about Adlek’s promotions, news or services.
  2. To produce internal statistics to improve our service.

If you do not want us to use your data for secondary purposes, email us at the address in section 7. Opting out does not affect the service you request.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use one client’s data to benefit another client.

3.WhatsApp and other Meta platforms

WhatsApp

We communicate with you through the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates. When you message us on WhatsApp:

  • Meta processes messages under its own policies: WhatsApp Privacy Policy and WhatsApp Business Terms.
  • We receive your number, your profile name and the content of the messages you send us, and use them only for the purposes in this policy.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for purposes other than those described here.
  • We only send you messages initiated by Adlek (for example appointment reminders) if you gave us your consent. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying “STOP”.

Facebook and Instagram: Marketing API, Pixel and Conversions API

When a client connects their Meta accounts to our application, we access the data described in section 1 through the Meta Marketing API, only with the permissions the client grants, such as ads_management, ads_read, business_management, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_ads.

We use that data only to manage and measure that client’s campaigns. The client can revoke access at any time by removing our app from their account (see section 7).

4.How we share information

We share data only as needed to provide the Services: with the client the data belongs to, for reporting and lead follow-up, and with technology providers that act as processors, meaning they process data on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Business Platform, Marketing API, Pixel and Conversions API), to send and receive messages, run ads and measure conversions.
  • Google LLC, for analytics and ad conversion measurement.
  • CRM providers (for example GoHighLevel / LeadConnector), to store and manage our clients’ leads.
  • Hosting, database and infrastructure providers (for example Vercel and Supabase), to host websites and securely store our Services’ data and conversation history.
  • Transactional email providers, to send notifications.
  • AI service providers (for example Anthropic), when an automated assistant processes your messages to reply to you or to generate analysis, copy and reports. Only the data needed for each task is sent and, under our agreements with them, it is not used to train their models.

Some of these providers may be located outside Mexico. We do not make transfers that require your consent, except those allowed by article 37 of the LFPDPPP, those required by a competent authority, or when needed to protect people’s rights and safety.

5.Retention and security

We keep your data for as long as it is needed for the purposes described and for the periods required by law. After that, we delete or anonymize it. In particular:

  • Campaign and performance data is kept as reporting history while the client relationship is active.
  • Lead data is kept for up to 24 months after the end of the client relationship, unless the client asks us to delete it sooner or the law requires otherwise.
  • Data received from Meta is deleted when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was obtained for, or when access is revoked, as required by Meta’s Platform Terms.

We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards: access restricted to authorized staff and by role, encrypted connections (HTTPS), two-factor authentication, encrypted backups, access tokens stored outside of source code, and only the minimum permissions needed. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

6.Your rights and choices

Under Mexican law (ARCO rights) you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Rectify it if it is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Cancel it when you believe it is not being used in line with this policy.
  • Oppose its use for specific purposes.

You can also revoke your consent or limit the use or disclosure of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address in section 7 with:

  1. Your name and the phone number or email address you contacted us with.
  2. An official ID, yours or your legal representative’s.
  3. A clear description of the right you want to exercise and the data it refers to.

We will reply within 20 business days and, if the request is granted, carry it out within the following 15 business days.

Other choices

  • Ads and cookies: you can control ad personalization in Facebook and Instagram (Settings & privacy → Accounts Center → Ad preferences) and in your Google account, and block or delete cookies in your browser.
  • Marketing messages: reply “STOP” to messages, or use the unsubscribe link in emails sent by our clients.
  • California residents: you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising in exchange for money.

7.Data deletion

You can ask us at any time to delete your data and your conversation history, including data we received through our Facebook / Instagram (Meta) app and our CRM integrations.

Option 1 — By email

  1. Email adlekcontact@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include your full name and the email address or phone number you contacted us with. If you are a business client, add your business name and the ad account or Page ID.
  3. If you contacted one of our clients (for example, you filled out a form on one of their pages), tell us the business name if you know it.

Option 2 — By WhatsApp

Send the message “DELETE MY DATA” to the same number you chatted with us on.

Option 3 — Remove our app from Facebook

If you connected our app to your Facebook account or business:

  1. Go to Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites or, for business assets, Meta Business Settings → Integrations → Connected apps.
  3. Find our app and select Remove.

Removing the app stops any further access. To also delete the data we already received, email us as in Option 1.

Timing

We confirm receipt of your request within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 20 business days. When it is done, we send you a confirmation with a reference code. We may ask you to verify your identity before deleting data, to protect you from fraudulent requests.

What we delete

  • Your contact and lead information (name, phone, email, form and chat answers) and your conversation history stored in our systems.
  • Data received from Meta related to you or your business assets, including stored access tokens.
  • Event and analytics records that can be linked to you.

We may keep only the information the law requires us to keep (for example billing records) or that is needed to resolve disputes, and only for as long as required. Aggregated and anonymized statistics that cannot identify you are not affected.

Data held by other companies

If you contacted one of our clients, that business is also responsible for the data in its own CRM; we will forward your request to them. Data collected directly by Meta or Google is controlled by those companies: you can delete it from your Facebook, Instagram or Google account settings.

8.Cookies and similar technologies

Our website and the pages we manage for our clients may use cookies, the Meta Pixel, Google tags and similar technologies to work properly, for statistical analysis and to measure ads. These may collect IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, click identifiers (such as fbclid and gclid), UTM parameters, cookies such as _fbp and _fbc, and event identifiers used to avoid counting the same conversion twice.

You can block or delete them in your browser settings and control personalized ads as described in section 6.
